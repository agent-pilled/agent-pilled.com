Approach

Review, choice and control

  • Independent review

    AI Agents is designed so the author cannot approve its own work. Required review applies to the specific result, with human approval where your rules require it.

  • Tools you choose

    Choose supported agent tools, models and providers per role. Deploy on infrastructure you control, with permissions defined for each role.

  • Free open-source core

    The AI Agents core stays free under Apache-2.0. Paid enterprise capabilities are planned for administration, audit, reporting, policy deployment and support.
